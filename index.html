Annual oil boiler servicing

A yearly check-up is the best way to keep your boiler safe, efficient and reliable. Here's what it does for you.

  • Keeps you safe

    Helps prevent oil leaks, breakdowns and carbon monoxide risks.

  • Saves you money

    A well-tuned boiler burns less oil, which can lower your heating bills.

  • Helps it last longer

    Regular servicing keeps your boiler running reliably for years.

  • Keeps your warranty valid

    Many manufacturers require an annual service.

  • Peace of mind

    One less thing to worry about when the weather turns.
